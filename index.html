Chapter IV

Walls you can
inspect yourself.

IV

  • Every session is sealed with AES-256-GCM or ChaCha20 over WireGuard. Keys rotate on their own and are never written anywhere. If one were somehow taken, it would open nothing that came before it.

  • We do not record which sites you visit, when you connect, or from where. There is no quiet exception for "diagnostics". Servers run from memory and forget everything on restart, and independent auditors confirm it every year.

  • If the tunnel drops for even a second, the gate shuts. Nothing leaves your device until the wall is back up. DNS, IPv6 and WebRTC leaks are sealed by default, not buried in a settings menu.

  • Some networks hunt for VPNs and block them. Stealth mode wraps your connection so it looks like a plain visit to any website. The gate opens, and what walks through is yours.

  • Our apps are open source. Anyone can read them, and every year we pay professionals to break them. Their reports go up unedited, including the parts that embarrass us.
